The appointment behind
the performance.

Sporting organisations operate under unusual decision pressure.

Time is limited. Information is fragmented. Reputation carries enormous weight. Past performance is highly visible. Internal expectations are intense.

Under these conditions, confidence can easily be mistaken for decision quality.

A successful appointment requires more than identifying an impressive professional.

It requires understanding whether the decision is coherent with the role, the organisation, its context and the risks surrounding the appointment.

Personal networks influencing selection
Recent results driving the decision
Urgency compressing the decision process
Internal politics shaping criteria
Intuition replacing structured analysis

RATIO METHODOLOGY

The decision begins with the role.
Not with the candidate.

Before assessing a person, RATIO structures the decision itself.

01
Understand the decision
What must this role actually accomplish? In this organisation, under these conditions, at this precise moment? The answer defines the perimeter of everything that follows.
02
Understand the role
Not the formal job description. The real leadership challenge the role demands in this specific context — distinguishing what is indispensable from what is merely desirable.
03
Understand the context
Which organisational conditions will facilitate or limit the new appointment? What could cause an apparently exceptional professional to fail precisely here?
04
Examine the assumptions
Which assumptions about the role are supported by evidence? Which are inherited from past appointments or unverified organisational habits?
ONLY THEN
Assess the person
The candidate is evaluated against the real decision the organisation must make — not against abstract criteria or internal assumptions.

WHAT RATIO DOES

Independence changes the question.

RATIO occupies a fundamentally different position from traditional intermediaries.

RATIO does not
Recruit candidates
RATIO has no interest in placing a specific candidate. We represent no one in the market. We hold no candidate relationships that could influence our analysis.
RATIO does not
Represent candidates
We have no agency relationships, no exclusive agreements with professionals, no financial interest in any specific outcome. Our sole interest is the quality of the decision.
RATIO does not
Replace the organisation's decision
The appointment remains entirely the organisation's. RATIO does not decide who should be selected. The club, the board, the ownership group retains full decision authority.
RATIO does
Structure the decision before it is made
The organisation identifies the appointment and the candidate or shortlist. RATIO analyses the role, context, decision criteria and candidate decision patterns — making the risk surrounding the decision more visible before commitment.
